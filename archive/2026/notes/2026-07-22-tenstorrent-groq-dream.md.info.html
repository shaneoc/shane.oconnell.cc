The Tenstorrent-Groq Dream

Date: 2026-07-22
Time: 7:50 am

Just woke up from a dream that I partially remember.

In the dream, I was working for Tenstorrent. I had just started working there after previously working for Groq, and had also already worked for Tenstorrent before working for Groq, so it was actually my second time working for Tenstorrent. I felt depressed. I was not actually doing any work and I felt bad about the fact that I kept changing jobs. For some reason the Tenstorrent office was in an old house.

While I was in the Tenstorrent office, at one point someone told me that an announcement was about to be made about the Tenstorrent IPO. I started going to the room where the television was located in order to watch the announcement, though I never arrived there and instead the dream changed. I found myself at a computer in another part of the house, and I was showing people something impressive on the computer screen. It was some kind of visualization that looked cool. Even though I was showing it to people, at first I could not remember what it was, though I knew it was something related to work. People were acting like it was something impressive that I had made, which made me uncomfortable because I was not sure if I had made it. I started saying it was something impressive that I had seen at Groq, until I remembered that it was actually something that [redacted] had shown me after I had started at Tenstorrent. Since I had just started at Tenstorrent, I did not know the people very well, and I felt embarrassed about seemingly showing off something as if I had made it even though someone at Tenstorrent had shown it to me after I had started.

I went into another room, and in the room I saw someone and told them that I wished that the government was paying my salary, so that I would not be obligated to do any work, and was just hanging out with them as friends. The person I was chatting with seemed like they understood and thought it made sense, though they made a joke about the fact that I was referring to people as friends. I still felt depressed, so I decided that I wanted to go home. I saw [redacted] and asked him how I could connect to the work network using a VPN. We had a conversation, though he did not explain how to connect using a VPN.

After this, I went to the kitchen, and I started to realize that I was actually just dreaming. The kitchen had people in it from both Tenstorrent and Groq. [redacted] was there, and so was [redacted]. Since I had realized that it was a dream, I decided to try to take control of the dream. I told people that I wanted female friends. I left the house with a bunch of the women who were in the kitchen, and we started walking somewhere else. I think we were walking towards the place where I lived.

On the way, the people that I was walking with started walking quickly, and I lost track of them. I went around a corner, and walked through a bunch of trees. I could see the people that I had been walking with in the distance. Then, suddenly, as I emerged from the trees, everything looked different. The sky was red, with clouds that were tinted red, and it was very hot outside. I could see lots of dead trees without any leaves, and I could no longer see any living trees. In front of me was a hospital and some other official looking buildings. I could see ambulances driving to and from the hospital. There were large fans around the parking lot next to the hospital blowing air on people to cool them down. After this, I woke up.

Interpretation

In my description of the dream, I mentioned two specific companies, Tenstorrent and Groq, which are real companies that I have interacted with. There is some background information on my interactions with these companies on the page on my website which describes how I am trapped in my apartment.

The Visualization That Looked Cool

Elements of the dream seem like they might have been metaphors for things from my real life. I suspect that the visualization that looked cool was actually a metaphor for the paper that Intel published in which my name had been removed. Even though the fact that my name was removed from the paper appears to indicate that I was disliked at work and therefore had negative experiences, it is arguably impressive that I managed to do something which caused someone to want to remove my name from the paper, which ended up being published on the official Intel website. I think that the fact that my name was specifically removed actually kind of shows that I did in fact make specifically important contributions. Therefore, the fact that my name was removed from the Intel version of the paper is actually something that I should theoretically be able to tell people about and use to improve my reputation, and I think I was recognizing this in the dream.

I suspect that the feeling that I had that I had worked for Tenstorrent previously before working for Groq was actually due to the fact that I did a job interview with Tenstorrent in 2019, which is when I was shown the paper with my name removed. I also knew a bunch of people who left the team that I was on at Intel to work at Tenstorrent around that time, and so for a long time I felt like I had a connection to the people that worked there. Both Tenstorrent and Groq hired people that used to be on the team that I was on at Intel.

The Government Paying My Salary

I suspect that the reference to the government paying my salary, and therefore not being obligated to do any work, was probably because I have been thinking about economics and the impact of artificial intelligence lately. I have a page on my website which I created recently where I have been trying to describe ideas for an economic system which makes work optional. Creating a system like this is something that seems necessary to me if artificial intelligence and robots are able to automate all work. This seems required due to the fact that many people would not be able to compete with automated systems for jobs in order to justify receiving money in order to pay their living expenses.

In real life, I have been starting to suspect that it may be the case that, over time, corporations have increasingly become social clubs in which many people are not actually doing useful work, and are instead engaged in complicated rituals designed to fill time and make it appear that their work is required. It seems like this would naturally occur as more and more work is automated over time. It seems like this would be particularly likely to occur for executives and higher level managers, as they have an incentive to hire people to perform all job functions, which from their perspective is similar to automation, however it seems like it could occur for any employee in the corporation. The rituals designed to fill time would naturally start to emerge as a way for people to continue to justify receiving salaries in order to pay their living expenses. Unfortunately, this means that over time, as more job functions are automated using technology, the only people who are left with jobs are people who are willing to participate in the rituals even though they are a waste of time, and are willing to mislead people into believing that they are doing something useful. At this point, I personally suspect that there are large groups of people inside corporations that actively work together to mislead people outside of their social group, and that when they are "working from home", they are actually doing no work at all.

The solution that I have come up with to solve this problem is to create an economic system which provides everyone with the basic things that they need to live, like shelter and food, for free, so that they are not being threatened with being physically harmed if they do not have a job. It should be possible to provide those things for free if we have sufficient automation, so that no human labour is required in order to provide them. If we had a system like this, it would be possible for people to be more honest about the nature of corporations and the fact that many of the employees at corporations are actually treating the corporation like a social club rather than a place were people are rewarded based on merit and actual productivity. With the current system, which does not have sufficient automation in order to provide shelter and food for free, people have an incentive to keep the fact that they are not doing useful work secret in order to avoid being one of the people forced to do unpleasant jobs which cannot currently be automated.

With the system that I have been envisioning, even though work would be optional, it seems likely that people would still want to be included in some kind of social club. It is possible that corporations could still perform the function of being social clubs. Some of the employees might still be doing useful work, however many of them could just be there just for the social aspect, and be expected to generally support the corporation in its activities without having any specific expectations of doing work. They could still participate in the rituals that they were used to, almost like the corporation was a kind of voluntary cult. One way to support this system might be for the government to actually pay corporations for providing people with a social environment. This is something that I have been thinking about recently, and I suspect that this is why in my dream I referred to the government paying my salary and therefore removing the obligation for me to do any work, while still being considered part of the corporation and still able to socialize with the people in the corporation.

Personally, I feel that CAMH, the psychiatric facility that I was recently forced to visit, is a good example of a corporation that has evolved in such a way that many of their employees appear to be engaged in rituals rather than doing useful work. Theoretically, I was supposed to have been brought to CAMH for the purpose of trying to improve my mental health. However, no attempt was made by CAMH to do anything to improve my mental health. Instead, they only engaged in the ritual of forcing me to visit and keeping me confined for multiple days before releasing me. I am starting to suspect that, rather than assisting people with mental health issues, CAMH has evolved to become a place where people are arbitrarily confined in order to meet a quota and allow CAMH to justify continuing to receive government funding. It seemed like the unpleasant nature of the experience was intentional, and was designed to provoke people into responding violently, which would then allow CAMH to justify their confinement. I suspect that I was only released because I remained calm during the experience despite being provoked.

Even though I have mentioned Tenstorrent and Groq in this description, I am not trying to imply that I have any specific reason to believe that they have an above-average number of people engaging in unnecessary rituals and pretending to work. They may have people doing that, however at least in their case they are not unnecessarily confining people against their will to preserve their jobs as CAMH appears to be doing.

The Hospital

The end of the dream, after I emerged from the trees and I saw the hospital, is a vivid memory. At that time, I was aware that I was dreaming, though I did not feel like I was controlling the dream. The dead trees without any leaves were prominent and very noticeable, and the red-tinted clouds were very detailed. The feeling that I had when I was looking at the scene was that I was looking at an image of the future, after climate change had caused the weather to permanently become warmer and caused the trees to die. It seemed like the hospital was there because the harsh environment was bad for the health of humans, and was causing people to have health issues requiring hospital visits.

After I woke up and thought about it, it seemed like there were a few things that probably influenced the end of the dream. For one, I actually have a fan in my real bedroom which I usually leave turned on while I am sleeping. I might have been partially aware of this during my dream, which might be why I saw the large fans at the end of the dream. The weather in Toronto has also actually been unusually hot recently, and this might have been a reason why the dream ended with concerns about climate change. In addition to that, my recent involuntary visits to psychiatric facilities without any justification or explanation, which were unpleasant experiences that I have been thinking about, might also have influenced my dream and caused me to imagine a hospital.

The fact that I was thinking about climate change right after thinking about people at corporations participating in unnecessary rituals and pretending to work is interesting in another way, as well. One side effect of the current system seems like it would be that it would slow down technological progress. It seems like it would cause a lot of people who could otherwise work on technology to address climate change to instead be forced to find a job from an existing corporation, which might not be doing anything to address climate change, in order to pay living expenses. There would also be employees at corporations that currently depend on receiving revenue from work that is harmful to the environment to have an incentive to continue that work, similar to how CAMH appears to have an incentive to arbitrarily confine people. If people had the flexibility to quit their jobs without worrying about paying for living expenses, it seems like there would probably be more people working on solving the problem of climate change.

Female Friends

The fact that I was trying to find female friends in the dream seems pretty clearly to be because I am trying to find a female friends in real life. At the point in the dream when I told people that I wanted female friends, I was aware that I was dreaming. I was trying to take control of the dream and have a positive experience.

When I wrote the first version of the description of the dream, I actually referred to the women that left the kitchen with me as "people who were female". I decided to change the phrase to the word "women" because I wanted to emphasise that they were actually women and that the group did not include males that just had feminine qualities in some way. However, for the last paragraph I decided to refer to them as "people" because by this point in the dream, the fact that they were female was not being emphasized. I also did not want to make it seem like the negative description at the end of the dream was associated with the fact that they were women, and make it appear that I have negative emotions that are associated with women. I was not sure where the women had gone at the end of the dream, and I was actually not thinking about them at that point. I was mostly thinking about the scene that I was looking at, and in particular I remember looking at the clouds, and thinking about how vivid and clear they looked with their red tint.

In retrospect, even though I was not thinking about it during the dream, it may be that this part of the dream was actually also a metaphor for my concern that my recent involuntary visits to psychiatric facilities, like CAMH, might be something that could harm my reputation, and make it more difficult for me to make female friends. Perhaps this is part of why I found myself looking at a hospital rather than being with the women that I was trying to walk with after I left the kitchen at the Tenstorrent office.

I also suspect that the fact that in the dream I told former coworkers that I wanted female friends might be related to the fact that I personally find it frustrating that engineering is a male-dominated profession. It is difficult to find a girlfriend when you spend most of your time working with men.

Final Thoughts

I still really want a girlfriend, and to be honest one of the main things that I have been thinking about while writing this description is how what I have written might be interpreted by any women that might read it. As I mentioned above, I am worried about my reputation being harmed as a consequence of the visits that I have been forced to make to psychiatric facilities, and I am also worried that the fact that my name was removed from the Intel paper might be a sign that my former coworkers have been harming my reputation as well. I would like to improve my reputation. I am pretty sure that I am completely sane and do not have a mental illness. In addition, I feel that I am an easy-going, laid back person. I have a dating profile on my website. I also eventually want to find employment, preferably working somewhere where I would have female coworkers.

I am obviously a bit frustrated right now about a few things that have happened to me recently, as I described on the page about being trapped in my apartment. Even though I have described my situation in detail on the webpage, and many people are aware of it, most people, including former coworkers, are either ignoring my messages completely, or even if they are answering, do not appear to be concerned about the fact that I am effectively trapped, or that I have been forced to visit psychiatric facilities. With regards to my former coworkers, I am not sure if this is because they have been pretending to work and are trying to hide it from me, however it seems possible to me. It would be cool if I had a girlfriend who also was frustrated about the same things and with the same people that I am frustrated with. It would be nice to have someone that was on my side.
