Process: -------- 1. Get credit report 2. Visit various new car dealerships and test drive the basic model cars. Find out: - make, model, year - financing (APR, term, monthly payment, is there any penelty for paying more each month then you're supposed to or for paying off in full anytime) - extra dealer fees - cost of extended warranty 3. Visit various used car dealerships and test drive some cars. Find out: - make, model, year, ___VIN number___ - financing (APR, term, monthly payment, is there any penelty for paying more each month then you're supposed to or for paying off in full anytime) - extra dealer fees - whether or not the car is sold "as is" - cost of extended warranty 4. If some of the new cars are somewhat reasonably priced, find out dealer information at carcostcanada.com. If not and there's some potential used cars, use their VIN numbers to find information from carproof.com. 5. Investigate extended warrantys from third-parties, http://www.warrantydirect.ca/index.asp?url=cbtcanada. 6. Investigate financing from bank 7. Choose a car, potentially re-test drive some maybe, and then try to make an offer. When buying car: - know cost of car insurance for that car beforehand - have credit score - have cost of extended warranty from online - have MSRP - have cost at car at other dealerships? - (used) do not buy car "as is", need at least 30 day warrenty and also extended warrenty from somebody - possibly take dealer car loan to get rebate, but then immediately pay off in full using another car loan
