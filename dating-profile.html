Food

Recently, while observing the genocide in Gaza on television and the reaction to it by others in society, I felt very frustrated about the fact that it is apparently possible for huge numbers of people to participate in something that is obviously unethical. I imagined the people in Israel and being in their situation, and it seemed likely to me that they were probably very influenced by peer pressure and being in a media environment that was telling them that they were doing something acceptable. After thinking about it a bit, I realized that this description could also probably apply to killing animals for food. It seems to me that many animals are probably conscious enough that they really are suffering a great deal as a consequence of our food production process. I realized that I might be one of the people doing something unethical because of peer pressure and the media environment and I did not like that. As a consequence, I would rather not participate in the killing of animals for food, and I would prefer to find a girlfriend who agrees with this point of view.

I personally enjoy the taste of meat a great deal, and so my solution is that I am now personally interested in improving the technology behind lab grown meat. I intend to avoid eating meat products until this technology is perfected and I can replace all meat with lab grown meat. If I can find a job improving this technology I would be happy and eager to do the work.

I also personally enjoy spicy food, and have a high tolerance for spice.
