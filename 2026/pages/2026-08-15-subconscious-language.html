Subconscious Language

First version: 2026-08-15
Last updated: 2026-08-25

Introduction

There are a bunch of places on my website where I have described the concept of what I have been calling the "brain hemisphere language" or the "subconscious language". In particular, I described the idea in this ChatGPT conversation, and I also described how I came to believe that the language might exist on the page about being trapped in my apartment. My theory is that there are subtle alternative definitions for a lot of words which we are usually only aware of in our right brain hemisphere and not our left brain hemisphere, and that we are actually using these alternative definitions to convey and interpret extra information when we speak and listen to language while not being fully aware of it. These alternative definitions form a separate, arguably secret language.

I am not sure which term I prefer. My thinking is that the term subconscious language makes it easier to intuitively understand how the language appears to be used and sounds a bit more normal, however I also kind of like the term brain hemisphere language because it makes it clear that the language appears to be designed to only be understood in one brain hemisphere and not the other. I also find the term brain hemisphere language somewhat amusing for some reason. They are both terms that I came up with myself, as I have never heard anyone else talk about this language before.

On this page I will try to describe the words that I suspect might exist in the language, and the definitions that I suspect that the words might have. I could be wrong about any of this, as these definitions are based on vague feelings that I have had when trying to pay attention to the words that people use as well as words that I have used myself. I am sympathetic to the idea that I have been noticing patterns in language that are not really there, and I am not offended if someone tells me that they think I am incorrect, however I do think that the theory is scientifically plausible as a theory and that I am not being irrational by considering it to be possible. If these patterns really exist, maybe it is possible to use a computer, in particular some kind of large language model or statistical algorithm to prove whether or not these things that I suspect that I have noticed are real patterns or not.

The Brain Hemispheres

The first step to convincing yourself that this language might exist is to first believe that it is possible that the two brain hemispheres might in some sense be operating almost like they are two different people. Personally, this YouTube video is what first convinced me that the brain hemispheres might be able to operate independently in some way. In the video, the author of the video says that only the left brain hemisphere is responsible for speech, however the author of the video appears to mainly be describing split-brain patients, in which the hemispheres have been surgically separated from each other. From what I have read online, it appears that in normal people both brain hemispheres have some language processing ability, even though the left hemisphere seems like it is more responsible for producing speech than the other. My theory is that while it may be that the left brain hemisphere is mostly responsible for speech, it might be the case that in a regular person who has not had their brain hemispheres surgically separated from each other that the right hemisphere is still at least able to contribute hints and influence speech in some way, even if it is to a lesser degree.

My theory is that from the perspective as the consciousness located in the left brain hemisphere that I feel like I am deciding all of the words myself using Broca's area, which is located in the left brain hemisphere, however this is not in fact completely true, and that often the words that I use have alternate meanings and are conveying information that has been provided by the right brain hemisphere which I am not normally able to perceive as the left brain hemisphere. As far as I understand it, the left hemisphere is more analytical and understands language in the form of rules, while the right hemisphere is more emotional and understands language in the form of metaphors. I suspect that when my right brain hemisphere influences my speech and word choices it inserts alternative meanings that are usually only perceived in the right brain hemispheres of the people that I communicate with, and that the right brain hemispheres of people actually coordinate as a group to prevent their left brain hemispheres from realizing that it is occurring.

It would be creepy and strange if this were true, however according to that YouTube video, the brain does have some strange behaviours in split-brain patients, and so it seems possible to me. I recommend reading the Wikipedia articles on split-brain patients, alien hand syndrome, dual consciousness and lateralization of brain function. The Wikipedia page on the left-brain interpreter is interesting because it describes the concept that the left brain hemisphere sometimes generates reasons after the fact for why it has done something that the right brain hemisphere is actually responsible for. This might explain why the left brain hemisphere believes that it has chosen all of the words that it uses even though the right brain hemisphere has actually subtly influenced the left brain hemisphere into choosing them when speaking.

The Numbers

Besides alternative meanings for words, I have a suspicion that numbers sometimes have specific alternative meanings. For numbers, the situation is a little bit different than the words above, because it is more often that I have simply tried to work out what probably rationally makes sense for alternative meanings for the numbers, rather than having seen a lot of examples. I am therefore a little bit less confident in their meanings than I am about the word pairs and the other words that I have listed above, for which I feel like I have seen a lot of examples in movies, television shows, song lyrics, and books.

However, it does make sense to me that numbers would often be used. It seems like they might be a good method of secretly transmitting information because there are many situations in which numbers are used in which people do not pay close attention to the specific numbers. With some specific numbers, like the numbers 6 and 9, I feel like I have seen some good examples of the numbers being used to indicate their alternative meanings, like in the name of the movie The Sixth Sense, as I mentioned previously, and so I am a bit more confident that they have an alternative meaning. They also have similar shapes, which is interesting, because it makes it seem like it they are meant to be grouped as a pair.

My theory is that the alternative meanings indicated by the numbers can be transmitted in a few different ways. The most obvious method is by sending the number itself. Alternatively, the numbers can be transmitted by using a word that has the number of letters indicated by the number, and I suspect that it is often the last word in a sentence that is most important when numbers are transmitted in this way. I suspect that it is also possible to transmit the numbers by grouping together a number of physical objects according to the number that is intended to be transmitted. This seems like a rational reason why numbers are good method of sending a secret message, because it does not require objects of any specific shape.

This makes it similar to the number 4 in the sense that it is related to information being transferred between the right and left hemispheres, however it seems like the number 4 is more often associated with a specific message which the left brain hemisphere might not be aware has been inserted, while 8 seems like it might be associated with a person who fully understands the subconscious language in both brain hemispheres and is aware of the messages in their left brain hemisphere. 2 and 3

I suspect that the numbers 2 and 3 are often used as generic ways of referring to things as either negative or positive, with 2 referring to something as being negative and 3 referring to something as being positive. I think of them as being similar to the comment voting system on Reddit, in which a downvote means that someone does not like a specific comment, and an upvote means that someone does like a specific comment. The numbers 2 and 3 seem like they might often be signalled to someone by using a specific number of physical objects, for example by handing someone two identical objects, you are sending a negative signal, however if you hand someone three identical objects, you are sending a positive signal. The fact that these numbers have low values makes it easier to send the signal without using a large number of physical objects, so this seems like a rational choice for the definitions of these numbers.

In addition to generically referring to something as being negative, the number 2 seems like it might also be used as a modifier for a number that comes after it to indicate that something is transitioning to being in the state of the second number, similar to the word "to". In these situations, the fact that the number 2 is present might not actually be referring to something negative. For example, if the numbers 2 and 3 come as a pair, as with the number 23, it means that something has transitioned to becoming a positive thing when it was not as positive before. If the numbers 2 and 5 come as a pair, for example with the number 25, it means that someone has become a friend. If the numbers 2 and 7 come as a pair, for example with the number 27, it means that someone has become family. It also seems to me like 2 might have a special meaning when it is the second number of a pair. The rule is a little bit complicated, but I have this feeling that when 2 is the second number, it has the same meaning as if the numbers were reversed while adding the number 2 to the other number. For example, 32 actually means the same thing as 25, and 52 actually means the same thing as 27. The reason for this is that 3, 5, and 7 seem like they might be relationship levels, and it is assumed that people usually transfer up to the next relationship level. If you were previously a 3, it is assumed that the next relationship level is a 5, and so 32 is a way of saying you increased from relationship level 3 to the next level, which is 5. It is also possible to use 2 in front of some of the other numbers which are not as associated with relationships. For example, 26 seems like it might be a way of saying that a message was changed into becoming a subconscious message when it was not one before.

As I mentioned previously, the numbers are sometimes hidden in messages by using words that have the corresponding number of letters. Interestingly, the word "to" itself has two letters. This means that there are a lot of ways to encode the 23, 25, and 27 number patterns using words by using the word "to" followed by a word with three, five, or seven letters. For example, if you say you are going to watch something on television, you have actually inserted the 25 number pattern by saying "to watch".

It is also possible for the number 2 to be doubled, as in the number 22, and this also seems like it has a specific meaning. The number 22 seems to me like it is associated with the word "reset". This can be interpreted as either a positive thing or a negative thing depending on the context. If you have previously been receiving a lot of 2s from someone, indicating that they have had an increasingly negative opinion of you over time, a 22 might actually be a positive thing because it means the relationship has been reset back to a neutral relationship. If you have previously been receiving a lot of 3s from someone, indicating that they have had an increasingly positive opinion of you over time, a 22 might be a somewhat negative thing because it means the relationship has been reset to being less positive. However, to be honest, it seems to me like the language overall is more focused on positivity, and it is not really possible to send an extremely negative message, so receiving a 22 is not a really negative thing even if you had been receiving a lot of 3s previously. These are words just like in a regular conscious language, and so interpreting them depends on the context.

Because they are a pair, I suspect that 2 and 3 are also sometimes used to refer to the brain hemispheres, however to be honest I am not completely sure which number is for which brain hemisphere, unlike the numbers 6 and 9 for which it seems more clear to me.
